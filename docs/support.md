Please include your device model, iOS version, Mira version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email medical information, passwords, private API keys, or other sensitive information.

## Common steps

- If camera access was denied, open iOS Settings, select Mira, and enable Camera—or use Search instead.
- If a product cannot be found, verify the barcode and try searching by product and brand name.
- Product formulations and public databases can change. Always verify the current package and allergen label.
- To remove local information, use **Settings → Clear Scan History** or **Settings → Reset All App Data**.

Mira provides general food and nutrition information, not medical advice.
