# Mira Privacy Policy

Effective: July 19, 2026

Mira is designed to work without an account, advertising, or cross-app tracking. This policy explains what information the Mira iOS app uses, where it is processed, and the choices available to you.

## Information stored on your device

Mira stores your health-focus and dietary preferences, scan history, favorites, shopping-list items, recent searches, app settings, and limited scan diagnostics locally on your device. This information is used to personalize results and provide app features. Mira does not operate an account system or upload this local profile to a Mira-operated server.

You can clear scan history or reset all local app data from Settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to normal device-backup behavior controlled by Apple and your device settings.

## Camera and photos

Camera access is used for live barcode scanning and, when you choose photo scanning, capturing product-label images. Photo-library access is used only when you choose an existing image.

In App Store builds, product-label text recognition runs on the device. Mira does not send the selected image to an AI provider. Recognized product text may be used as a search query as described below.

## Product lookup requests

When you scan or search, Mira may send a barcode, product name, brand, or search terms to public product-information services, currently Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, to retrieve matching product and nutrition information. These services necessarily receive the request and standard network information such as your IP address. Their handling of requests is governed by their own privacy practices.

- [Open Food Facts privacy information](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy)
- [USDA privacy policy](https://www.usda.gov/privacy-policy)

Mira also includes a limited on-device product catalog that can answer some requests without contacting those services.

## Tracking, advertising, and sale of information

Mira does not include advertising SDKs, does not access the advertising identifier, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

## Data retention

Mira-operated systems do not retain the local profile described above because Mira does not receive it. Product-information providers may retain request records under their own policies. Local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset it in Mira, or remove the app.

## Children

Mira is a general-audience food-information app and is not directed to children under 13. Mira does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security and changes

Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy as Mira changes; the effective date above will identify the latest version.
